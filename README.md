Postwith is a social media scheduler you use from your AI chat. Tell ChatGPT, Claude or another AI what to post and
when. It makes a version for each of your social accounts, shows you every one, and posts on time only after you say
yes.

It works with:

- **Instagram** (professional accounts): photos, carousels of up to 10 photos, Reels and Stories
- **Facebook Pages**
- **Threads**
- **TikTok**
- **YouTube**
- **Pinterest**
- **LinkedIn** (your own profile)

## How it works

1. **Connect your accounts.** Sign in at [app.postwith.app](https://app.postwith.app) and connect each social account
   with the network's own sign-in. Postwith never sees your passwords.
2. **Add Postwith to your AI.** One link, in ChatGPT, Claude or another AI app (steps below).
3. **Ask.** "Post this video to Instagram and TikTok tomorrow at 9." Your AI writes each version, shows them to you,
   and schedules them once you say yes.

Things you can ask:

- "Turn this photo into an Instagram post and a Pinterest pin for Friday morning."
- "What's scheduled for next week?"
- "Any new comments on yesterday's Reel? Draft replies for me to check."
- "Which of my posts did best this month, and what should I post next?"

## Add it to your AI

| AI app | How |
| --- | --- |
| **Claude** (web, desktop, phone) | Open [Postwith in Claude's directory](https://claude.ai/directory/connectors/postwith) and choose **Connect**. |
| **ChatGPT** | Open **Plugins** in the sidebar, choose **Add › Add custom MCP server**, name it Postwith and paste `https://mcp.postwith.app/mcp`. Leave Authentication on OAuth, then sign in to Postwith. Works on Plus. |
| **Codex, Claude Code, Cursor, Gemini CLI** | See [Connect your AI](https://postwith.app/docs/) for each one. |

Want to try it first? Turn on **Practice mode** in Postwith and your AI can do everything except actually post.

## Nothing posts by surprise

- **You see every version first.** Your AI shows you each network's post in the chat and schedules it only after your
  OK.
- **Your choices stay yours.** For TikTok and YouTube, your AI asks who can see each post and whether a video is made
  for kids, and never answers for you.
- **Official sign-ins only.** No bots and no passwords. You can end your AI's access anytime in Postwith › Settings ›
  Your AI.
- **Never twice.** If a network doesn't answer, Postwith checks what actually happened before it tries again.

## Every post on record

Postwith keeps a record of every post: what it was, how it was made and how it did. It compares each post with its own
account's usual at the same age, so your AI can tell you what works for your audience and help you stop posting at
random. Results come from Instagram, Facebook, TikTok and Threads.

## For developers

- **MCP server:** `https://mcp.postwith.app/mcp` (Streamable HTTP, OAuth sign-in or an API key header). It's in the
  [official MCP Registry](https://registry.modelcontextprotocol.io/v0/servers?search=app.postwith/postwith) as
  `app.postwith/postwith`. Tools and setup: [postwith.app/mcp](https://postwith.app/mcp/).
- **REST API:** `https://api.postwith.app/v1`, with an OpenAPI description, and OAuth apps so your product can post for
  your users with their OK. [API docs](https://postwith.app/docs/api/).
- **CLI:** `npx postwith` ([npm](https://www.npmjs.com/package/postwith)).
